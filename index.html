About the Project

AgriSense AI is an innovative agricultural platform that combines Artificial Intelligence with sustainable farming practices. The system helps farmers detect weeds, identify crop issues, and receive eco-friendly recommendations.

Objectives

  • Reduce crop losses due to weeds
  • Promote sustainable farming
  • Reduce excessive fertilizer usage
  • Support precision agriculture
